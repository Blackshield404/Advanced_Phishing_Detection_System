Think before you click.

Phishing works by making ordinary requests look trustworthy. Follow four simple steps before trusting a suspicious message, link, or request.

01
!
FIRST STEP

Stop & Pause

Don't let urgency make the decision for you. Phishing messages often create fear, pressure, or excitement to make you act without checking.

⚠ Common pressure tactics "Act immediately" "Account will be suspended" "Payment required"
02
🔗
SECOND STEP

Check the Link

Before opening a link, look at where it actually leads. Pay attention to misspelled domains, unusual subdomains, strange paths, or unexpected destinations.

🔎 Look closely Misspelled domains Unexpected subdomains Strange URL paths
03
✓
THIRD STEP

Verify the Request

If a message asks for a password, payment, sensitive information, or an unexpected file, verify the request through another trusted channel.

🛡 Safer ways to verify Open the official app Visit the official website yourself Contact the organization directly
04
🔐
FINAL STEP

Protect Your Account

Good account security can reduce the impact of a successful phishing attempt. Use unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication whenever it is available.

🔐 Good security habits Use unique passwords Enable MFA Keep devices updated

Pause. Check. Verify. Protect.

A few seconds of caution can prevent a costly mistake.
