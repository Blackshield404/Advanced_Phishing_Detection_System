PHISHING ATTACK

Email Phishing

What is it?

Email phishing is a deceptive attack where criminals pretend to be a trusted company, bank, service, or person. Their goal is to make you click a link, open an attachment, or reveal sensitive information.

01 Fake email
→
02 Victim clicks
→
03 Fake website
→
04 Data stolen
REALISTIC EXAMPLE

"Your account requires immediate verification. Please verify your information to avoid suspension."

⚠ Watch for Urgency Unexpected requests Suspicious URLs
🛡 Stay safe

Verify the request through the organization's official website or app before entering sensitive information.

02
▣
MESSAGE-BASED ATTACK

Smishing

What is it?

Smishing is phishing delivered through SMS or messaging platforms. Attackers create messages that appear to come from banks, delivery services, companies, or other trusted sources.

01 Fake message
→
02 Link opened
→
03 Fake page
→
04 Information stolen
REALISTIC EXAMPLE

"Your delivery could not be completed. Confirm your address using the link below."

⚠ Watch for Unknown numbers Urgent messages Unexpected links
🛡 Stay safe

Avoid opening unexpected links in messages. Check delivery, banking, or account information directly through the official app or website.

03
◉
TARGETED ATTACK

Spear Phishing

What is it?

Spear phishing is a highly targeted form of phishing. Instead of sending the same message to many people, attackers use information about a specific person or organization to make the message more convincing.

01 Research target
→
02 Personalized message
→
03 Victim trusts it
→
04 Information stolen
REALISTIC EXAMPLE

"Hi Alex, please review the project document before today's meeting." — followed by a malicious link.

⚠ Watch for Unusual requests Personalized messages Unexpected files or links
🛡 Stay safe

If a request seems unusual, verify it through another trusted communication channel before responding or opening anything.

04
▦
QR-BASED ATTACK

QR Phishing

What is it?

QR phishing, also called quishing, uses a QR code to redirect people to a fraudulent website. The page may imitate a login, payment, or verification service and attempt to collect sensitive information.

01 Scan QR
→
02 Website opens
→
03 Fake page
→
04 Data stolen
REALISTIC EXAMPLE

A QR code on a fake payment notice sends you to a counterfeit payment page asking for your login or payment details.

⚠ Watch for Unknown QR codes Unexpected payments Unfamiliar websites
🛡 Stay safe

Check the destination website after scanning a QR code and avoid entering sensitive details if the destination looks unfamiliar.
